# Changelog

Released versions of open-exchange from 0.28.0. Earlier releases are described by their release commits
and tags (`git log`, `git tag -n`).

## 0.28.0 (npm tag `next`)

On skein-amm 0.55.0: LP fees are paid straight to the LP's wallet.

- The token image installs skein-amm v0.55.0 (was v0.52.1): the pool contract's layout 3, two LP fee
  addresses in every pool's state (LpSatsPkh, LpTokenPkh), Swap paying the LP fee to the one for its
  direction, the affirmation message v3; the amm-lookup and amm-validator programs follow.
- The swap pages are rebuilt from skein-amm v0.55.0's web/ui: no Collect button, no "Available to collect";
  the `openex fees` basket is gone from the pages' manifest. The wallet takes each LP fee in when it catches up
  with the pool (`syncFees`).
- The host's swap relay reads the pool code of skein-amm v0.55.0 (host/pool-code.ts regenerated by
  scripts/pool-code.sh, tag v0.48.1 → v0.55.0); without it the commission rule would refuse every swap on a
  0.55.0 pool.
- Package on @dcasegr/amm-engine 0.55.0; the skill, its examples and wallet-headless.md on amm-engine /
  amm-client 0.55.0; the skill no longer describes `availableFees`, `collectFees` or the fees basket.
- build.zig.zon on skein-amm v0.55.0 (apps/swap/bin/config.wasm unchanged); swap app 0.28.0.
- The acceptance's fixture pools are written in layout 3 with message v3.
- The skill, its examples and wallet-headless.md on `@bopen-io/color-permission-module` 0.2.3 and
  `@1sat/actions` 0.1.6 (the versions skein-amm 0.55.0's pages and client pin).

Upgrading: pools made under 0.27.x are no pools to 0.28.0, and the upgrade installs the new token app into
every token instance. Before a host upgrades, its LPs collect their fees and close their pools with 0.27.x;
or the host is relaunched fresh. Hosts on 0.27.x and 0.28.0 do not recognise each other's pools.

Published under the npm dist-tag `next`; `latest` stays 0.27.1. Install with `open-exchange@0.28.0`.
